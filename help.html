Quick guide

Help

Shortcuts for the parts of YouTube Music you use most.

Playlist actions

On a playlist page, hover a track to show your selected quick actions. On touch screens they stay visible. Remove, Queue, and Play next use YouTube Music’s own track menu, so an action can be unavailable for playlists you can’t edit.

Side play opens a separate YouTube Music player in a dialog. It pauses the current player and attempts to resume it at the same position when you close the dialog. This is experimental: YouTube Music may block playback in an embedded frame.

Sidebar shortcuts

Shortcuts are paths within music.youtube.com. Add a name and path in settings, for example /library/playlists. The Downloads shortcut is a starting point; if that page is unavailable for your account, remove it or replace the path.

Not available yet

Keep the save-to-playlist picker open is saved as a preference but is not connected to YouTube Music’s picker yet. Opening playlists, artists, and channels in dialogs, moving the player to the top, and preserving the original queue for a separate side-play session need further live-site testing.

Report a broken control

YouTube Music changes its page markup over time. If a shortcut stops working, use the site’s native control as a fallback and reload the YouTube Music tab after updating the extension.
